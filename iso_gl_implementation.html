Why select multiple packages?

Transition periods — Support renewals mid-transition when some policies were written under a prior effective date.
Audit & run-off — Keep older packages available for reproducing historical premium calculations without contaminating current content.
Content version binding — Every quote and policy is pinned to the exact package version used at inception, regardless of what is current today.
